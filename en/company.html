Mission

Find it, explain it, solve it

We make the sound around a problem visible as data, so you can see what happened and act on it. We started with noise complaints. The same method now covers industrial safety.

Find

We look for problems that keep coming back, in complaints and on site. Noise is the largest category of environmental complaints in Korea, yet few records exist to judge it.

Explain

We record sound type, level, location, and time, and show the cause of the problem with numbers.

Solve

We deliver alerts, camera linking, and data for policy decisions, in forms teams can use on site right away.
