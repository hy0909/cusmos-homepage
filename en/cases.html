Case 01 · Siheung City

A motorcycle noise pilot in Baegot

Motorcycle noise complaints kept coming back on Baegot Haesong-simni-ro. We installed sensors there and logged the cause and timing of each noise. The pilot ran on observation and guidance only, with no enforcement.

Case 02 · Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO)

Sound alerts linked to CCTV at the Taean thermal power plant

At the Taean plant, our sensors pick up hazards that cameras cannot see, and the alert links to CCTV monitoring. We developed the technology and delivered it to KEPCO. In a proof of concept (PoC), we measured what adding sound data did for CCTV monitoring.
