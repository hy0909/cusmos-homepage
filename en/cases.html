Case 01 · Siheung City

Baegot motorcycle noise pilot

Motorcycle noise complaints kept coming back on Baegot Haesong Sipli-ro. We installed units there and recorded the cause and timing of each noise. We ran the pilot with observation and guidance only, with no enforcement.

Case 02 · Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO)

Sound-linked safety monitoring at Taean thermal power plant

At the Taean plant, we detect hazards that cameras cannot see and link sound alerts to CCTV monitoring. We developed the technology and delivered it to KEPCO. In a proof of concept (PoC), we measured what adding sound data did for CCTV monitoring.
