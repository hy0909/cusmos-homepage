Microphone array

We set microphone spacing for each site

We do not use one array for every site. Spacing and signal processing are chosen by which sounds you need to catch and where.

Wide spacing

Better at telling the direction of distant and low sounds. Suits wide roads and open areas.

Narrow spacing

Captures high-pitched and nearby sounds precisely. A smaller unit that fits tight installation spots.

Mixed spacing

Combines wide and narrow spacing for sites where several kinds of sound happen at once.

Edge processing
