02 · Microphone array

We set the microphone spacing for each site

Spacing set for each site.

We do not use one array design everywhere. We choose the spacing and signal processing based on which sounds you need to detect, and where.

  1. Wide spacing

    Better at judging the direction of distant and low sounds. Suits wide roads and open areas.

  2. Narrow spacing

    Picks up high-pitched and nearby sounds precisely. The smaller unit fits tight spots.

  3. Mixed spacing

    Combines wide and narrow spacing for sites where several kinds of sound occur together.

03 · Edge processing

Analyze on site, and send only what matters

About 95% less data, by calculation.

Sending all data to a server makes transmission and storage costs keep growing. Our sensors analyze on site and send only the results and the raw audio from the moment of an event.

The figure is calculated from 16 kHz, 16-bit mono raw audio. It assumes only 5% of the raw audio is sent, along with the analysis results. This is not a measured saving. Actual communication costs depend on your plan and operating conditions.

05 · Fixed stations vs CUSMOS

Fixed stations are few and manual

Noise is the largest category of environmental complaints in Korea, at 46.5% in 2024. Yet 1,766 of the 1,828 sites in Korea's national noise network are still measured by hand.
