2. Scope

This policy covers personal information collected through this website: the pages you visit, the contact form, and the cookie choice banner. It does not cover the sensors and software we install at customer sites. Customer contracts and their data processing terms govern that data.

3. Information we handle

3.1 Contact form

The contact form asks for your name, work email, organization, role, area of interest, and message. The website does not send these fields to any server. When you press the button, your own email app opens with the fields filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send in that app. Your email then arrives in our mailbox, and we use it to reply.

Giving us this information is up to you. If you do not send an enquiry, we cannot reply to it.

3.2 Technical data when pages load

When a page loads, our hosting provider, GitHub, receives your IP address, the page you requested, the time of the request, and details about your browser. The news, notices, and brand identity pages also read their content from Supabase, our database provider, which receives your IP address when those pages load. This website does not write any visitor data to Supabase.

If you open the start address without a language folder, your browser asks Cloudflare (cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/trace) and GeoJS (get.geojs.io) at the same time which country the visit comes from. Both services receive your IP address. The site uses only the country code to send you to the matching language version and does not store it. If neither service answers in time, your browser language settings decide.

3.3 Your cookie choice

If you answer the cookie banner, your browser stores your choice and the date in its local storage under the name cusmos-consent. This choice stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

6. International transfers

CUSMOS is based in the Republic of Korea. Our hosting provider and database provider process information outside Korea, mainly in the United States. The transfers rest on the following:

  • Korea. The European Commission (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2022/254) and the United Kingdom (The Data Protection (Adequacy) (Republic of Korea) Regulations 2022) have decided that Korea protects personal data adequately for companies covered by the Korean Personal Information Protection Act. CUSMOS is such a company.
  • GitHub. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension. GitHub also relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914). Check GitHub's certification.
  • Supabase. Supabase's data processing addendum uses the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers. Its server region is to be confirmed.

8. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies. It has no analytics, advertising, or social media tracking. It does not let third parties follow visitors across websites. Fonts and photos are loaded from our own server, so your browser does not contact Google or Unsplash for them.

This website does not track visitors over time or across other websites. For that reason, it does not change its practices in response to browser "Do Not Track" signals. If we add analytics or embedded media later, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

You can change your cookie choice at any time.

9. Your rights

9.1 All visitors

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and ask us to restrict how we use it. You can withdraw any consent you gave us at any time. Where the law gives you the right, you can ask for a copy in a portable format.

9.2 EEA and UK visitors

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR, you can object to processing based on our legitimate interests. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

9.3 California residents

The California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, gives you the right to know what personal information we collect, to ask us to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not collect sensitive personal information through this website. The categories we collect are identifiers (name, work email, and IP address), professional information (organization and role), and the content of your message. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. We apply the same request process to all visitors.

9.4 How to make a request

Send an email to To be confirmed with "Privacy request" in the subject line. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act. We reply within one month, or within 45 days for California requests.

10. Children

This website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.
