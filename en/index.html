How it works

Listen. Locate. Alert.

Each sensor analyzes sound on the device. Only the information you need goes to the server.

01

Listen

Small edge AI sensors on existing poles listen all day, every day. No staff needs to be on site.

02

Locate

Several microphones pick up the same sound. The sensor works out where it came from and AI names the type: motorcycle, horn, construction, or equipment fault.

03

Alert

Your control room gets an alert on screen, and the nearest camera turns toward the spot. Every event is logged as evidence for complaint handling and policy.
