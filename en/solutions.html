01 · Smart city

Smart city noise monitoring

Place a sensor where noise complaints keep coming back. It records what caused the noise, when, and where. Officers can then choose enforcement hours and places, and decide where to make facility improvements, without a crew at every complaint.

Residents

See how often night noise happens on your street, and how it compares with other areas.

Local government

Set enforcement times and places, and choose facility improvements, based on records.

Policy makers

Build living-noise indicators from the data and measure whether a policy works.

Main targetsMotorcycle noise, construction noise, car horns, and everyday noise complaints
Install locationsExisting poles, such as CCTV and vehicle-detection poles
Dashboard outputsLive monitoring, sound records by site and hour, and complaint response reports
02 · Industrial safety

Industrial safety sound detection

CCTV only sees where its lens points. Our sensors listen across a wider area. When they hear an accident sound or an equipment fault, they locate it and turn the nearest camera toward it.

Korea's Serious Accidents Punishment Act now reaches workplaces with fewer than 50 employees. Catching an incident fast and keeping a record of the response matters more than ever.

03 · Ultrasonic camera

Handheld ultrasonic camera

When a sensor flags a sign of trouble, an inspector takes this camera to the site. It overlays the spots where ultrasound is coming from onto the view of the equipment, so the fault is easy to find.
